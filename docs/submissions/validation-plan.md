# Demand validation plan

Status on 9 October 2026: no customer interviews, users, revenue, or funded pilots, confirmed by the owner. This document is a plan; it is not evidence of completed outreach or traction.

## Initial audience

Payment reviewers at small agencies or contractor-heavy teams already paying through EVM wallets/stablecoins. Interview the person responsible for checking recipient details and approving invoices.

## Five discovery conversations

Ask for a walkthrough of the last contractor payment, using redacted data. Ask how recipients are registered, how changes are verified, who approves, how revisions are handled, and what records are retained. Ask about actual past mistakes without suggesting that fraud must have occurred. Learn which tools they already use and which step consumes the most time.

Ask to observe a PayFence review using sample/test data. Confirm recording consent separately; never request customer wallet keys or move real funds during discovery.

## Pilot hypothesis

Seek up to ten willing teams after discovery. Test a review-only workflow first. Success means users can identify a changed recipient, understand approval invalidation, complete a second review cycle, and find the evidence useful. Do not claim real-loss prevention from a synthetic exercise.

| Measure | How to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Correctly detected exceptions | Known changed-recipient/duplicate/over-limit sample cases |
| Review completion and confusion | Observed steps and user explanations |
| Time per reviewed invoice | Measured start/end under comparable conditions |
| Repeat usage | A second agreed review session; distinguish commitment from completed use |
| Willingness to pay | Open-ended discussion followed by a specific proposed pilot price |
